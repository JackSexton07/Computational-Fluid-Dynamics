Geometry: nose leading edge 0.17 m above ground (Corvette 0.09 m); underbody 0.146 m at mid-wheelbase
(Corvette 0.114 m).

Interpretation: the air dam, chin and splitter are designed to reduce front lift, and the model shows that.
About -0.085 of the nose's pressure lift comes from stagnation on the closed grille and intake surfaces,
which don't exist on the real car (air flows through the radiator into the engine bay). The real car's
cooling flow would also raise pressure under the hood and front underbody, adding front lift. So the model
overpredicts front downforce; the real car most likely has small front lift or close to zero.
