Findings:
- The duct now flows (0.9 m3/s at about half the freestream speed) and the duct system's
  front lift fell by ~0.06 (front-axle share +0.076 -> +0.015): it works as a downforce device.
- It did not reduce drag: the duct system's drag rose slightly (+0.014). The inlet still stagnates
  and the air leaving the vents upward into the flow over the bonnet adds drag.
- The splitter underside is unchanged at Cp +0.32 (Cl +0.156, front-axle +0.193). It sits below the
  splitter plate, so the duct does not affect it. It is now the biggest single source of front lift.
- Net averaged effect: front Cl -0.030, total Cl -0.011, Cd +0.011. The front axle still lifts.
